The Story

Four friends from the hills of Northwest Arkansas, playing rock the way it was meant to be played — loud, honest, and live.

Coming Up Aces is a four-piece classic rock band forged from friendship and a shared love of the classics. Drawing deep from the well of Southern rock legends like Lynyrd Skynyrd and the heartland storytelling of Tom Petty, then running it through the raw, unfiltered energy of grunge, Coming Up Aces has carved out a sound that feels both timeless and alive.

You'll find us in bars, on festival stages and anywhere across Arkansas and Oklahoma — wherever there's a room that wants a real rock band.
